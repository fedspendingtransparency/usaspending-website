What does retirement look like to you?

Maybe it’s traveling across the country, starting a business, spending more time with family, or simply having peace of mind about the years ahead. Retirement looks different for everyone, but planning for it often involves more than personal savings.

Did you know federal programs also play an important role in supporting millions of Americans?

Whether retirement is right around the corner or still years away, understanding the programs that help provide financial security can offer valuable insight into the bigger picture.

While [USAspending.gov](https://www.usaspending.gov/) isn’t a personal financial planner, it provides the public with transparent data on federal spending, including programs like Social Security. By exploring spending trends and how federal dollars support these programs, you can gain a better understanding of the government's role in supporting Americans during retirement and beyond.
