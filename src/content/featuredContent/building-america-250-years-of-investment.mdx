Taking a trip this summer? Have you ever visited a museum, national monument, or even a highway rest area and thought “who helps to pay for all of this?”

The answer (sometimes) is you!

Whether you’re visiting an American icon like the Hoover Dam, traveling to a different state on a highway, or watching a tiny version of Earth from outer space thanks to NASA satellites, your tax dollars are a part of America’s investments in our shared future.

There are a couple ways to find out more about how U.S. government spending works, and how your tax dollars contribute to different things you might see on a summer trip:

1. Visit [USAspending.gov](https://www.usaspending.gov/search) and search by keyword using a term like “[national monument](https://www.usaspending.gov/search?hash=becce1d812889becfee7ed433a1a9abe)” to see government spending on the maintenance, restoration, and improvement of monuments throughout the country.
2. Visit the U.S. [Fiscal Data](https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/) website’s [Federal Spending](https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/americas-finance-guide/federal-spending) page to get a bigger picture of the nation’s federal revenue, spending, deficit, and more.

## Exploring America’s Investments
This summer marks America’s 250th Birthday. Since 1776, our country has been investing in its infrastructure, its technology, and its people. Let’s explore some of America’s investments and learn how government spending contributes to them.

### Monuments of Progress
Consider the [Hoover Dam](https://www.usbr.gov/lc/hooverdam/faqs/faqs.html), completed in 1936 during the Great Depression. This concrete arch-gravity dam on the border between Nevada and Arizona harnessed the Colorado River and brought electricity to the Southwest. The [$49 million](https://hoover.archives.gov/hoovers/hoover-dam) federal investment (about $1 billion today) also irrigated farmland and employed thousands when jobs were scarce. It still powers [1.3 million](https://www.usbr.gov/lc/hooverdam/faqs/powerfaq.html) homes annually. 

Did you know that you can see what the government spends today to maintain and operate the Hoover Dam?  Use the [keyword search](https://www.usaspending.gov/search?hash=3136c4053b3ea489eee820a182f53e6d) (the “filter by keyword” field) on USAspending.gov and see for yourself. 

The [Interstate Highway System](https://highways.dot.gov/highway-history/interstate-system), launched in 1956, represents one of our boldest investments: $500 billion over decades. These 48,000 miles of road transformed commerce, connected communities, and made cross-country travel accessible to ordinary Americans. Economists estimate it returns $6 for every dollar invested.
