Taking a trip this summer? Have you ever visited a museum, national monument, or even a highway rest area and thought “who helps to pay for all of this?”

The answer (sometimes) is you!

Whether you’re visiting an American icon like the Hoover Dam, traveling to a different state on a highway, or watching a tiny version of Earth from outer space thanks to NASA satellites, your tax dollars are a part of America’s investments in our shared future.

There are a couple ways to find out more about how U.S. government spending works, and how your tax dollars contribute to different things you might see on a summer trip:

1. Visit [USAspending.gov](https://www.usaspending.gov/search) and search by keyword using a term like “[national monument](https://www.usaspending.gov/search?hash=becce1d812889becfee7ed433a1a9abe)” to see government spending on the maintenance, restoration, and improvement of monuments throughout the country.
2. Visit the U.S. [Fiscal Data](https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/) website’s [Federal Spending](https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/americas-finance-guide/federal-spending) page to get a bigger picture of the nation’s federal revenue, spending, deficit, and more.
