The reasons for visiting [USAspending.gov](https://www.usaspending.gov/) are as unique and varied as the users themselves. Many of our users have specific, personalized searches they have carefully crafted to help them find the exact government spending data they’re looking for.

In our feature, “My USAspending Search,” we highlight one of our users and their unique approach to searching government spending open data.

Meet Alicia Wilson-Ahlstrom, Co-Director for Research Capacity at the Children’s Funding Project.

In our [Your Data, Your Story video](https://youtu.be/PKFYRaSGKOc?si=slFKt3Z9mHQ2WA76), Alicia talks about using USAspending to track public funding for children and youth at the federal level. Alicia’s organization uses Advanced Search features like <GlossaryLink term="fiscal-year" label="Fiscal Year" />, <GlossaryLink term="location" label="Location" />, and <GlossaryLink term="place-of-performance" label="Place of Performance" /> to find relevant federal spending data. Features like USAspending’s [Custom Award Data](https://www.usaspending.gov/download_center/custom_award_data) helped Alicia to download bespoke data specifically related to children and youth programs.

Using USAspending data, the Children’s Funding Project created a small database with more than 280 <GlossaryLink term="passistance-listings-cfda-program" label="federal assistance listings" /> and has used the data on federal obligations to help them with their mission.

“The platform does a wonderful job of having a lot of information to educate users who want to know more about how federal spending works,” Alicia said.

She added that USAspending might be particularly useful for those who “want to delve into a particular assistance listing to understand how exactly it might be landing in their communities or how it might be utilized to support children and families in their communities.”

Want to start your own USAspending Search? Try a keyword search for a term like “[education](https://www.usaspending.gov/search?hash=b3c0f780c1ae95686b5529a03d9b414b)” and filter by [Location](https://www.usaspending.gov/?about-the-data=location-filter) to find spending in your state or community.
