The reasons for visiting [USAspending.gov](https://www.usaspending.gov/) are as unique and varied as the users themselves. Many of our users have specific, personalized searches they have carefully crafted to help them find the exact government spending data they’re looking for.

In our feature, “My USAspending Search,” we highlight one of our users and their unique approach to searching government spending open data.

Meet Alicia Wilson-Ahlstrom, Co-Director for Research Capacity at the Children’s Funding Project.

In our [Your Data, Your Story video](https://youtu.be/PKFYRaSGKOc?si=slFKt3Z9mHQ2WA76), Alicia talks about using USAspending to track public funding for children and youth at the federal level. Alicia’s organization uses Advanced Search features like Fiscal Year, Location, and Place of Performance to find relevant federal spending data. Features like USAspending’s Custom Award Data helped Alicia to download bespoke data specifically related to children and youth programs.
