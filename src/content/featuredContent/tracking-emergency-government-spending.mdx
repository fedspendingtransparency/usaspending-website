When disaster strikes, whether hurricanes, wildfires, floods, or public health emergencies, the federal government provides financial assistance to help communities respond and recover.

But how does this funding happen?

This funding happens through several coordinated steps involving Congress, federal agencies, and local agencies.

![how-emergency-funding-happens](tracking-emergency-government-spending.png)

## How Emergency Funding Happens
1. **Congress appropriates funds**: After a major event, Congress may pass emergency supplemental appropriations bills to make additional funding available beyond what’s included in the federal budget. These laws designate money for disaster response, recovery, and mitigation activities.
2.	**Funds are managed by federal agencies**: Agencies like the Federal Emergency Management Agency, U.S Department of Housing and Urban Development, and the Small Business Administration manage these funds and distribute money where it’s needed most through disaster relief grants, housing assistance, low-interest loans and more.
3.	**Codes are assigned to track funding**: Beginning with the CARES Act in March 2020, the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) assigned a code to identify each of the six laws passed during the COVID-19 pandemic. These codes, known as <GlossaryLink term="disaster-emergency-fund-code-defc" label="Disaster Emergency Fund Codes" /> (DEFC), are used to track the spending funded under each legislation.
4.	**Transparency through [USAspending.gov](https://www.usaspending.gov/)**: Federal agencies report their financial activity to USAspending.gov in accordance with the DATA Act, so the public can see how federal dollars are used to support disaster-related efforts. People can use the [Advanced Search](https://www.usaspending.gov/search) tool on USAspending to search for this data.
## How to Search for Emergency Funding on USAspending.gov
1.	Navigate to [Advanced Search](https://www.usaspending.gov/search)
2.	Under DEFC, select the disaster or emergency you’re interested in
3.	Apply filters for <GlossaryLink term="agency" label="agency" />, <GlossaryLink term="recipient" label="recipient" />, state, or award type to narrow down the results
4.	You can also use the keyword search function at the top of Advanced Search to type in the name of the disaster or emergency, like ‘Hurricane Katrina,’ to see related spending

Transparency during times of uncertainty builds trust and accountability. The data on USAspending.gov empowers people to follow federal spending to ensure that emergency funding reaches the people and places that need it the most.
