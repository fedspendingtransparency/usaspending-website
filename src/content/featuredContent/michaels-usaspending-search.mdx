The reasons for visiting USAspending.gov are as unique and varied as the users themselves. Many of our users have specific, personalized searches they have carefully crafted to help them find the exact government spending data they’re looking for.

In our feature, “My USAspending Search,” we highlight one of our users and their unique approach to searching government spending open data.

Meet Michael LeJeune, a professional government contracting coach with RSM Federal.

Video Goes Here

In our [Your Data, Your Story video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c-bqfpWSSrI), Michael shares how he uses USAspending.gov to help business owners navigate the government market and find out who’s buying what his clients are selling.

Michael appreciates that USAspending’s Advanced Search has a flexible user interface with different filters and fields, so he can adjust his searches in real time.

“I like how I can change the fields, and thus change my report, on the fly,” Michael said.

While conducting their market research, Michael and his clients use the Spending by Category tables in their search results to view a list of the top Agencies or Awarding Sub-Agencies from highest to lowest. They can also toggle over to the Recipient table to view a list of the top recipients, or visit other tables for information based on NAICS or PSC codes or Assistance Listings.
