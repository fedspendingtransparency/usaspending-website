Starting October 1, 2026 (FY27), the Republic of Palau, Federated States of Micronesia, and Marshall Islands will appear on USAspending.gov as foreign countries rather than U.S. states or territories. This change follows federal geographic naming standards and ensures the site reflects current federal classifications.  

The update will be applied to all historical data, so reporting is consistent for awards both before and after October 1, 2026. In Award Search, these locations will be removed from the “U.S. States and Territories” list and will appear under their country names. While awards to these countries make up less than 0.005% of all awards, this update ensures our data remains accurate and consistent across the platform.
