The Appendix to the President’s Budget (PB) outlines yearly agency spending plans. To establish a consistent approach to program activity reporting in alignment with the PB, the [Office of Management and Budget](https://www.whitehouse.gov/omb/) (OMB) has introduced a unique identifier: Program Activity Reporting Key (PARK).  

Beginning in Fiscal Year 2026, PARK replaced the Program Activity Code (PAC) and Program Activity Name (PAN) as the primary identifier for reporting program activities, helping improve the accuracy and consistency of data across federal agencies. OMB is the authoritative source for PARK codes, which are organized by OMB Fund Families and correspond to specific activities or projects detailed in the program and financing schedules of the annual United States Government budget.  

PARK codes also play an important role in mapping reported program activity data to the information displayed on USAspending.gov. PARK introduces a standardized reporting structure; an existing PAC or PAN may not always have a direct one-to-one relationship with a PARK code.  

Although PARK is used for current program activity reporting, beginning in FY26, PAC and PAN remain available for prior-period resubmissions. This allows agencies to resubmit or correct historical data using the program activity information that was applicable to the original submission period.
